Marketing Dashboard

Interactive Sales & Marketing Dashboard with KPIs, revenue tracking, traffic sources, conversions and campaign analysis.

Problem

Marketing performance data (revenue, traffic sources, campaigns, conversions) is usually spread across exports and reports that are hard to compare side by side or track over time.

Objective

Build a single dashboard that shows revenue and conversion trends, traffic-source performance, and campaign results together — so priorities are obvious at a glance.

Approach

  • Structured raw marketing/sales data into an analysis-ready table
  • Built Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts to compare campaigns and channels
  • Used Power Query to consolidate and clean multiple data sources
  • Designed a KPI-first layout for quick executive review

What I Built

An interactive Marketing Dashboard covering revenue trends, traffic-source breakdowns, conversion rates and campaign comparisons — shown in the video walkthrough above.

Key Insights

Breaking traffic and conversions down by source made it easy to see which channels were driving the most value, and campaign-level Pivot Charts highlighted trends that were invisible in the raw export.

Outcome

A reusable marketing-reporting template that demonstrates how raw campaign data can be turned into a clear, decision-ready dashboard.

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables Power Query Dashboard Design
